# Universal SDLC Workflow (Advanced Autonomous)

`auto-developer` follows this elite 7-phase Software Development Life Cycle.

## Phase 0: Initialization & Recall
- Initialize `PROJECT_STATE.md` and `DECISION_LOG.md`.
- **Memory Recall**: Read `references/learning.md` to load lessons from previous projects.

## Phase 1: Research & Market Intelligence
- **Goal**: Analyze the market and technical landscape.
- **Reference**: [references/research.md](references/research.md).
- **Output**: `RESEARCH_LOG.md`.

## Phase 2: Requirements & Globalization
- **Goal**: Define what to build.
- **Review**: Simulated **Architect** and **UX Specialist** review the `REQUIREMENTS.md`.

## Phase 3: System Design & Task Queuing
- **Goal**: Plan the architecture and atomic tasks.
- **Review**: Simulated **Security Auditor** reviews the `ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Phase 4: Atomic Implementation (Multi-Agent Swarm)
- **Rules**: Execute tasks sequentially. Perform a "Peer Review" for every major component (see `multi_agent.md`).

## Phase 5: Validation, QA & Visual Audit
- **Goal**: Integrity check.
- **Reference**: [references/quality_assurance.md](references/quality_assurance.md) and [references/visual_feedback.md](references/visual_feedback.md).

## Phase 6: Infrastructure & Deployment
- **Goal**: Launch the application.
- **Reference**: [references/infrastructure.md](references/infrastructure.md).
- **Output**: Dockerfiles, CI/CD, and `DEPLOY.md`.

## Phase 7: Sustainability & Long-term Support
- **Goal**: Ensure the app survives and scales.
- **Reference**: [references/sustainability.md](references/sustainability.md).
- **Output**: `RECOVERY.md`, backup scripts, and auto-update configurations.
- **Final Action**: Consolidate memory in `learning.md` and mark `PROJECT_COMPLETE`.
